Who We Are

A Movement Born
from Prayer

ARAN Prayer Movement is a Spirit-led Christian organisation rooted in Tamil Nadu, India. Founded on the conviction that prayer changes nations, we gather believers to intercede for individuals, families, communities, and the nation.

Under the passionate leadership of Pr. JAE. Muthu, ARAN stands as a beacon of hope — hosting monthly prayer gatherings, outreach missions, and community healing services. We believe every prayer prayed in faith moves the hand of the Almighty.

Our Leader

Pastor
JAE. Muthu

Prayer is not our last resort — it is our first response. Every burden we carry becomes lighter when laid at the feet of Jesus.

Pr. JAE. Muthu founded ARAN Prayer Movement with a deep burden for intercessory prayer and community transformation. With decades of ministry experience across Tamil Nadu, he has led countless souls to experience the healing and delivering power of Jesus Christ. His heart beats for broken families, lost youth, and communities in need of spiritual revival.

What We Do

Our Ministries

01

Intercessory Prayer

Our core calling — praying without ceasing for individuals, families, the church, Tamil Nadu, and the nation of India. Monthly all-night prayer gatherings are our heartbeat.

02

Healing & Deliverance

We minister to those in physical, emotional, and spiritual pain — praying for healing, casting out fear, and proclaiming freedom through the name of Jesus Christ.

03

Evangelism & Outreach

Taking the Gospel to villages, slums, and unreached communities across Tamil Nadu — sharing the love of Christ through word, deed, and demonstration.

04

Youth & Children

Equipping the next generation with the Word of God, prayer disciplines, and a passion for Jesus — building young intercessors who will shape tomorrow.

05

Family Restoration

Counselling and praying with broken families, offering God's hope, reconciliation, and restoration for marriages, parent-child relationships, and homes.

06

Social Care

Serving the poor, widows, orphans, and the marginalized in our communities — meeting practical needs as an expression of Christ's love in action.
